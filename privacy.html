Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · UniTax Technologies Global

1. Who we are

UniTax Pro is an enterprise accounting and financial-management product built for manufacturers and distributors of goods in India. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email, hashed password (never the password itself), designation, workspace reference, created/updated timestamps.
  • Workspace data — company masters, profit centers, stock items, ledgers, vouchers you post, remarks, and audit logs generated by the system.
  • Usage data — login timestamps, device/browser agent, session IP, feature-usage counts, error reports for reliability and fraud prevention.

3. How we use it

  • To operate, maintain and improve the product (authentication, voucher posting, reports, approvals).
  • To satisfy statutory and audit obligations — the system keeps an immutable audit trail of all changes.
  • To respond to support requests and send essential service messages (OTP, invoices, terms changes). We do not send marketing without your opt-in.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorized access.

4. Where it lives

Workspace and account data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (region: asia-southeast1). Device IP and timestamps are used solely to detect anomalous sign-in activity.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Google (Firebase/Cloud services) as infrastructure provider, under their data-processing terms;
  • Authorities, when legally required to comply with valid Indian law;
  • A successor organisation in case of merger or acquisition (with advance notice).

6. Your rights

You can: request a copy of your account data; request correction; request deletion (subject to statutory retention of accounting records under the Companies Act, 2013); withdraw consent for non-essential processing; raise a grievance with our Nodal Officer.

7. Retention

Account and workspace data is retained while your subscription is active. After cancellation, we retain accounting records for the statutory period (minimum 8 years under Indian law) and delete the rest within 90 days of cancellation.

8. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, signed sessions, least-privilege access controls, and audit logging on every mutation. No user other than the account holder can see their workspace data without explicit authorization.

9. Children

UniTax Pro is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this policy with reasonable notice (via in-app banner or email). Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
